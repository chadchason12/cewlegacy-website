The Story behind CEW Legacy Homes…

Decades ago, our dad, Carl, fell in love with homes. He spent years running his own business selling used mobile homes, then turned his focus to owning rental properties across Anderson County — always seeing potential where others saw just another old house.

When Carl passed away in 2020, our family made a decision: we weren't ready to let what he built end with him.

Today, CEW Legacy Homes is run by Carl's wife, Sue, his son, Mike, his daughter, Holly, and his son-in-law, Chad. The same way Carl left a legacy for us, we hope to carry on the legacy of every home we touch.
