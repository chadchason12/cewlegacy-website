Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Answers to what prospective tenants ask us most.

How do I apply for a rental?

Email us to ask about availability. If we have a home that fits your needs, we will send you a direct link to apply online. The application takes about 10 minutes and can be done from your phone.

Is there an application fee?

No. CEW Legacy LLC does not charge an application fee. There is no cost to apply.

Do I need to provide my Social Security number?

No SSN is required on the rental application form. We collect standard rental information such as your name, contact information, current address, and employment details.

Will I get a text message about my application?

Only if you choose to opt in. There is an optional SMS consent checkbox on the application form. Checking it is not required to apply. If you do opt in, you may receive 1 to 3 text messages about your application status.

How do I opt out of text messages?

Reply STOP to any text message we send and you will be immediately removed. You can also email us and we will remove you manually.

Do you manage properties for other owners?

No. CEW Legacy LLC manages only our own portfolio of properties. All homes we rent are owned directly by us, which means you always deal with the owner.
