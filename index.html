Who we are

Every house has a story worth keeping.

CEW Legacy has called Anderson County home since 2020. CEW Legacy Homes was formed in 2026 with one purpose: find houses across Palestine and the surrounding area that have been overlooked, and help them continue their legacy. We are not developers. We are a family that believes a well-cared-for home is one of the best things you can hand down.

Our mission

"We want to help Palestine's homes continue their legacy."

There are a lot of homes in East Texas that have good bones and real history — they just need someone willing to put in the work. That is what we do. We find them, we restore them, and we put them back into the community for families to build the next chapter in.
