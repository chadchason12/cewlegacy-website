Building On What's Already There

CEW Legacy brings thoughtful updates and fresh vision to homes with good bones — going room by room, trade by trade, until it's done right. The goal isn't a house that just looks good in photos; it's a home ready to carry on a legacy that started long before we got there.

Active Project
1305 E Park Ave
Palestine, TX · Anderson County

This charming 1942 home right on Park Avenue caught our eye with beautiful bones and a gorgeous yard. With 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, plenty of storage, and a separate dining room with original French doors, we're starting with a beauty. Our plan is to bring it up to the luxuries of 2026 without losing an ounce of its charm — new floors throughout, granite in the kitchen and bathrooms, new cabinets, and fresh paint from top to bottom. At 1,700 square feet in the heart of Palestine, this home is going to be a dream in just a matter of a few more weeks!
