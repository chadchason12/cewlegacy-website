Privacy Policy & SMS Terms

CEW Legacy LLC · Last updated August 12, 2026

Who we are

CEW Legacy LLC is a residential property management company based in Palestine, TX. We manage rental properties in the East Texas area. This page explains how we use your phone number and what we do with the information you give us when you apply for a rental.

SMS messages

If you apply for one of our rental properties and consent to SMS, we may send you a text message with a link to your application, an approval notification, or a rejection notification.

  • Message frequency varies — typically 1 to 3 messages per application.
  • Message and data rates may apply depending on your carrier plan.
  • To opt out, reply STOP to any message we send.
  • For help, reply HELP or email cewlegacyllc@gmail.com.

Information we collect

When you fill out a rental application, we collect your name, email address, phone number, current and prior address, employment and income information, names and relationships of anyone who will live with you, and vehicle and pet information you choose to provide.

We do not collect Social Security numbers, credit card numbers, or bank account information.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide only to evaluate your rental application and contact you about it. We do not sell your information. We do not share any personal information with third parties except as required by law or to verify information you provided.

Mobile phone number and messaging consent

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Your mobile number and SMS opt-in status are used solely by CEW Legacy LLC to communicate with you about your rental application.

Data retention

We keep application records for our internal records. If you would like us to delete your information, contact us at the email below.
