Privacy Policy

CEW Legacy Homes · Last updated September 2026

Who we are

CEW Legacy Homes is a family-owned house renovation company based in Palestine, TX. We renovate and sell single-family homes in East Texas. This page explains how we handle the information you share when you sign up to be notified about available properties.

SMS messages

If you submit our "Get Notified" form and consent to SMS, we may send you a text message when a renovated home matching your preferences becomes available.

  • Message frequency varies — typically 1 to 3 messages per available property that matches your stated preferences.
  • Message and data rates may apply depending on your carrier plan.
  • To opt out, reply STOP to any message we send.
  • For help, reply HELP or email info@cewlegacy.com.

Information we collect

When you fill out our property interest form, we collect your first and last name, phone number, and optionally your email address, bedroom preference, bathroom preference, and price range. We collect this information only to match you with properties that fit your needs and to contact you when one becomes available.

We do not collect Social Security numbers, payment information, or financial account details.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide only to notify you about properties that match your stated preferences. We do not sell your information. We do not share any personal information with third parties except as required by law.

Mobile phone number and messaging consent

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to any third party or affiliate for marketing or promotional purposes. Your mobile number and SMS opt-in status are used solely by CEW Legacy Homes to send you property availability notifications you requested.

Data retention

We keep your contact information as long as you remain opted in. If you would like us to remove your information, email us at the address below or reply STOP to any text message.
