SMS Verbal Consent Script

CEW Legacy LLC · Last updated September 2026

Purpose

This page documents the exact word-for-word script CEW Legacy LLC property managers read to prospective tenants before collecting a mobile phone number for SMS notifications. This script is used when a tenant contacts us by phone or in person rather than applying through our online form.

When this script is used

This script is read any time a CEW Legacy LLC property manager intends to send an SMS message to a prospective tenant who did not submit the online rental application. The manager reads the full script before recording the tenant's phone number and does not send any text message unless the tenant verbally agrees.

Word-for-word agent script

The agent reads this script in full — including the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions URLs — before asking for consent. No text message is sent until the prospective tenant says yes.

"Before I send you the application link, I want to let you know that CEW Legacy LLC may send you 1 to 3 text messages about your application — your application link, an approval, or a denial. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out, and HELP for assistance. You are not required to agree to texts to apply."

"You can review our Privacy Policy at https://cewlegacy.com/privacy and our SMS Terms and Conditions at https://cewlegacy.com/terms before you decide."

"Do you consent to receiving text messages from CEW Legacy LLC?"

Required disclosures included in this script

All required disclosures are delivered verbally to the prospective tenant prior to collecting consent:

  • Business name: CEW Legacy LLC
  • Message types: application link, approval notification, rejection notification
  • Message frequency: 1 to 3 messages per application
  • Rate disclosure: message and data rates may apply
  • Opt-out instructions: reply STOP
  • Help instructions: reply HELP
  • Consent is optional: not required to apply
  • Privacy Policy URL verbally disclosed before consent: https://cewlegacy.com/privacy
  • Terms & Conditions URL verbally disclosed before consent: https://cewlegacy.com/terms
  • Explicit verbal yes required before any message is sent

What happens after consent

The property manager records the tenant's phone number only after receiving a clear verbal yes. The first message sent is the rental application link. No further messages are sent unless the application is acted upon (approved or denied). The tenant may reply STOP at any time to stop all messages.
