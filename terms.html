SMS Terms & Conditions

CEW Legacy Homes · Last updated September 2026

Program description

CEW Legacy LLC (d/b/a CEW Legacy Homes) sends text messages only to individuals who have voluntarily submitted the opt-in form on our website and provided their mobile number to receive property availability notifications. Messages are limited to: notification that a renovated home matching your stated preferences is available or coming soon, and follow-up messages directly related to your inquiry.

How you consent

You consent to receive SMS messages from CEW Legacy LLC (d/b/a CEW Legacy Homes) by completing the "Get Notified" form at https://cewlegacy.com, providing your mobile phone number, and checking the SMS consent checkbox before submitting the form. Consent is not required as a condition of receiving information about our properties. You may also email us at info@cewlegacy.com to request information without providing a phone number.

Message frequency and rates

  • Message frequency varies — typically 1 to 3 messages per available property that matches your stated preferences.
  • Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan.

Opt-out

You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message from us. After opting out, you will receive one confirmation message and no further messages. To opt back in, reply START.

Help

Reply HELP to any message for assistance, or email us at info@cewlegacy.com.

No data sharing

Your mobile phone number and SMS opt-in consent are not sold, shared, or transferred to any third party or affiliate for marketing or promotional purposes. Your information is used solely by CEW Legacy Homes to notify you about properties that match your preferences.

No third-party marketing

CEW Legacy LLC does not engage in affiliate marketing, third-party lead generation, or any prohibited messaging use case. All messages are sent directly by CEW Legacy Homes and relate solely to property availability notifications you requested.
